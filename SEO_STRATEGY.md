\# Antarctic Pulse — SEO Strategy



\## Project Purpose



Antarctic Pulse / Pulso Antártico aims to establish long-term organic authority around:



\- Antarctica

\- Patagonia

\- Southern Ocean

\- Polar geography

\- Maritime routes

\- Antarctic logistics

\- Southern Chile

\- Puerto Williams

\- Punta Arenas

\- Antarctic access
