\# Antarctic Pulse — Brand Guidelines



\## Brand Identity



Antarctic Pulse / Pulso Antártico is a polar documentary and editorial media brand focused on:



\- Antarctica

\- Patagonia

\- Southern Ocean

\- Maritime routes

\- Polar science

\- Southern geography

\- Antarctic access

\- Cartographic storytelling



The identity must feel:

\- serious

\- modern

\- scientific

\- cinematic

\- documentary

\- geographic

\- internationally credible



\---



\# Core Brand Personality



The project should communicate:



\- geographic authority

\- polar atmosphere

\- editorial credibility

\- strategic southern perspective

\- documentary realism

\- cartographic intelligence



Avoid looking like:

\- a generic travel influencer brand

\- a tourism agency

\- a patriotic propaganda channel

\- a sensationalist news page

\# Social Media Guidelines



All social profiles should maintain:

\- consistent handles

\- consistent bios

\- coherent color palette

\- linked ecosystem references

\- professional visual hierarchy



Recommended phrase:

“Part of the Austral Beacon media network.”



\---



\# Website Direction



Websites should prioritize:

\- readability

\- maps and structure

\- editorial layouts

\- fast loading

\- SEO readiness

\- mobile-first responsiveness



Preferred design:

\- dark/light contrast

\- subtle grid overlays

\- cartographic textures

\- minimal motion



Avoid:

\- excessive animations

\- cluttered layouts

\- generic blog themes



\---



\# Long-Term Brand Goal



Position Antarctic Pulse as:

\- a respected polar documentary platform

\- a geographic and maritime media reference

\- a southern gateway narrative platform

\- an authoritative Antarctic editorial ecosystem
